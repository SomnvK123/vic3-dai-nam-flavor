At the same time, it optimizes their production and construction logic, making them less likely to go bankrupt and develop better economies. In addition, to simulate the establishment of AI Italy and Germany, special national scripts are added to them, and exclusive buffs are added to AI Japan to successfully complete the Meiji Restoration and for the United States to surpass Britain as the world's leading superpower at specific times. Finally, to ensure that AI achieves historical territories, corrections will be made through specific logs.[/b]
